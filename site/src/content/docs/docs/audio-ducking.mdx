---
title: Audio ducking
description: Abe can lower other apps' volume while you record, then restore it.
---

Audio ducking turns down music, videos and calls while you're recording, so they don't drown you out or end up in your transcription. When you stop, the volume comes back.

## Setting the level

**Settings → Advanced → Recording → Audio Ducking** is a slider for how loud other apps should be while you record:

| Setting | Effect |
|---|---|
| **20%** (default) | Other apps play at a fifth of their volume |
| **Off** (100%) | Nothing changes |
| **Mute others** (0%) | Other apps go silent while you record |

## Platform notes

- **Windows:** Abe lowers the volume of other applications individually and leaves its own sounds alone.
- **macOS:** Abe lowers the overall system output volume instead, then restores it.
- **Linux:** support depends on your audio system and may be unavailable.

:::note
If Abe is force-quit or crashes while recording, other apps might stay quieter than before. Restore them in your system's volume mixer.
:::
