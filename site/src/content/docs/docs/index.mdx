Abe is a free, open-source voice-to-text app for your desktop. Hold a hotkey, talk, and your words appear wherever your cursor is. Transcription runs either **locally** with Whisper on your own computer, or in the **cloud** with your own API key. There are no accounts and no Abe servers.
