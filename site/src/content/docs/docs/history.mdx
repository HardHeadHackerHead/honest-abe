title: History
description: Find, copy, edit and export your past transcriptions. History is stored only on your computer.
---

Abe keeps your recent transcriptions in a local history so you can find something you said earlier.

## What you can do

- **Search** across everything you've dictated.
- **Copy** any entry back to the clipboard.
- **Edit** recent transcriptions from the main window. If you correct a word, Abe offers to add it to your [word dictionary](/docs/dictionary).
- **Export** your history as **TXT**, **CSV** or **JSON**.
- **Clear all** to delete everything.

Each entry shows its duration and word count. Press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>Alt</kbd> + <kbd>V</kbd> to paste your most recent transcription again without opening the app.

## Storage and privacy

- History is stored **only on your computer**, in the app's local storage. It's never uploaded or synced.
- Abe keeps your **500 most recent** transcriptions; older ones are removed automatically.
- Clearing history or uninstalling Abe deletes it.

## Usage stats

The **Usage** view summarizes your recent sessions for transcription and Voice Transform. Like history, these stats are computed and stored locally.
