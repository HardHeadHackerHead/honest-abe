---
title: Word dictionary
description: Teach Abe the names, jargon and spellings it gets wrong, and it fixes them automatically.
---

Speech recognition sometimes mishears names, product terms or jargon. The word dictionary fixes them automatically: whenever a transcription contains a word or phrase you've listed, Abe replaces it with your correct version before inserting the text.

## Add an entry

1. Open **Settings → Word Dictionary**.
2. Under **Misheard word**, type what Abe wrote, for example `cue ball`.
3. Under **Correct word**, type what you meant, for example `Kuball`.
4. Add it. From now on every transcription gets the fix.

You can edit or delete entries at any time.

## Learn from your corrections

When you edit one of your recent transcriptions in the Abe window, Abe compares your edit with the original. If you changed a word, it offers to **Add to dictionary?** so the same mistake is fixed next time.

## How matching works

- Matching ignores case, so `cue ball`, `Cue ball` and `CUE BALL` all match.
- Abe keeps the capitalization of what was said: if the misheard word started a sentence with a capital letter, the replacement does too. All-caps stays all-caps.
- Entries can be single words or short phrases.

## Tips

- Add the way the word is actually being misheard, not just a likely spelling. Check your history to see what Abe wrote.
- If one name is misheard several different ways, add an entry for each.
- The dictionary is stored only on your computer.
